Purrfect Cat Tangle

A tangle of tails.
A cozy catnap.

Move cats between cushions, untangle colorful tails, and solve your way to a cozy catnap.

Google Play ↗App Store ↗

Android and iOS releases are currently under review on Google Play and the App Store. Store pages may become available after approval.

01

Untangle their tails

Drag a cat to an empty cushion, or tap a cat and its destination. Follow the crossings and find the move that loosens the tangle.

02

A little more to think about

Match cats to colored cushions, free tails held by burrs and plan around move limits in puzzles with up to four cats.

03

Make room for a nap

Hints and boosters offer a helping paw. Enjoy gentle music and sleepy celebrations when a puzzle is solved.
